# LeePerrySmith.glb

Lee Perry-Smith head scan by Infinite Realities (ir-ltd.net), as distributed
with the three.js examples.

Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Changes made here: the mesh is not modified. `components/head3d.py` renders
it to a shaded raster from a fixed dorsal viewpoint and draws the 10-20
electrode positions over it as vector marks. The resulting head images appear
in Figure 1 of the paper, whose caption carries the attribution.
