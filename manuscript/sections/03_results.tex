\subsection{What each procedure buys}
Table~\ref{tab:main} gives the source-only references and the effects. Chance differs across the three datasets, so the reference accuracies are not comparable to one another; every claim below is a within-subject difference against an arm's own reference. Supervision rejects on all three datasets and alignment on two of three, by amounts that do not carry across them, and the ordering between the two reverses.

Alignment is worth $+1.58$\,pp on 2a, where it does not survive correction (7 of 9 subjects, Holm $p=0.18$), against $+8.07$ and $+9.12$\,pp on BNCI2014-002 and PhysionetMI, where it does (12/14 and 93/105, $p\le0.005$). Target supervision runs the other way: $+9.45$\,pp on 2a, helping every subject, falling to $+6.52$ and $+4.89$\,pp on the larger cohorts. Comparing the two directly---exploratory, prompted by this ordering rather than specified in advance---supervision leads alignment by $+7.87$\,pp [$+4.95,+11.03$] on 2a, is indistinguishable from it on BNCI2014-002 ($-1.55$ [$-4.46,+0.89$]), and trails it by $-4.23$\,pp [$-5.84,-2.64$] on PhysionetMI. Both endpoints reject under the decision rule---Holm-adjusted within these three tests, $p=0.008$ and $p<0.001$---so the two datasets are not scattered around a common value. Being exploratory, this is an observed reversal in these runs, not a confirmed ordering.

The calibration budget is confounded with the dataset, $|C_s|$ differing 6.4-fold, so we re-ran the arms that consume it with $|C_s|$ capped by a nested stratified subset and $T_s$ untouched. Shrinking it moves the effect monotonically but only a little: at a common budget of 45 trials the three datasets read $+5.88$, $-2.62$ and $-4.23$\,pp, so the ordering holds and the budget accounts for about $2$ of the $12$\,pp separating 2a from PhysionetMI. The full curve is in the supplement.

\subsection{Does it survive a different decoder, or a chronological split?}
Two things could make these effects artefacts of our setup rather than of the protocol. The random split interleaves the halves in time, so they share drift and attention state; BCI IV 2a's two sessions let the same 288/288 budget be partitioned chronologically, and over five runs the effects barely move (\textsc{sup}${}-{}$\textsc{ea} $+7.75$\,pp [$+5.54,+10.29$] against $+7.87$), making the contrast cross-session there. And the decoder is our own reimplementation, so we repeated the grid with braindecode's \texttt{EEGNetv4} (0.8.1, matched hyper-parameters) swapped in: source-only accuracy moves $-0.81$, $+0.27$ and $-0.55$\,pp, so the low reference belongs to zero-calibration evaluation rather than our code, and the reversal reproduces at $+8.27$\,pp on 2a and $-4.65$ on PhysionetMI. \textsc{ea} and \textsc{sup} reproduce wherever they reject; \textsc{sel} on BNCI2014-002 does not. These runs and the capped-budget ones carry their own source snapshots.

Whether the two compose is undetermined: the interaction $(\textsc{ea+sup}-\textsc{ea})-(\textsc{sup}-\textsc{src})$ is $+2.62$, $-3.45$ and $-1.33$\,pp, none rejects, and per run its sign is consistent within each dataset but not between them. Its sign may be dataset-dependent, and none of this is evidence of additivity.

\subsection{The aggregation unit}
Holding the predictions fixed and changing only the grouping, the resampled null matches the finite-population prediction to within $0.0013$ and tracks the trials per group, though it also depends on the pooled accuracy and the group count. With 288 test trials each on 2a it is $0.029$ against $0.177$ by true subject, a factor of $6.2$, falling to $2.4$ with 80 trials and $1.8$ with 45; the supervised arm behaves likewise ($6.99$, $2.96$, $2.23$), though not at the same size, so the gap is not independent of training.

That null is a benchmark, not a decomposition: shuffling removes the subject structure along with the trial noise. Estimating the two components separately instead, under a conditionally binomial model, puts finite trials at $2\%$, $17\%$ and $29\%$ of the observed between-subject variance and leaves standard deviations of $0.174$, $0.111$ and $0.113$ against raw values of $0.177$, $0.122$ and $0.134$; the estimator, its assumptions and the per-run terms are in the supplement. Raw figures rank PhysionetMI as the more heterogeneous cohort and corrected ones do not, though that gap is unresolved ($+0.002$ [$-0.031$, $+0.024$]). The control itself establishes only that spread exceeds the benchmark everywhere ($p<0.001$).

\subsection{The two arms off the grid}
Choosing the checkpoint on $C_s$ can cost accuracy: \textsc{sel} is $-1.69$\,pp [$-2.80,-0.55$] on PhysionetMI, helping 37 of 105 subjects, surviving correction ($p=0.009$) and reproducing under the reference decoder ($-1.81$)---and that is where the selection set is smallest, 45 trials against the 1872 it replaces. Its positive values elsewhere do not reproduce, so we claim only this direction. No \textsc{pool} arm rejects.
